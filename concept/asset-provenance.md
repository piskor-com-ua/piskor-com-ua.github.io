### Екстер’єр

Файли: `assets/house-concept.png`, `assets/house-concept.webp`.

Промпт:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: photoreal architectural hero visualization for a luxury Ukrainian architecture studio website design concept. Create one exceptionally refined contemporary low-rise villa nestled at edge of a forest, camera at eye height 3/4 view across natural grasses. Sophisticated horizontal volumes, charcoal stone, smoked floor-to-ceiling glass with subtle warm interior lights, pale travertine terrace, dark timber soffits, cantilever roof. Restrained European architecture editorial photography, believable exquisite details, overcast early evening, muted graphite blue-green forest and moss, natural stone warm neutrals. Cinematic wide landscape image 3:2 or wider. Building centrally placed so a vertical central crop still shows a strong architectural composition. Ground level path leads to entrance; mature pine trees frame both edges. Top quarter soft dark overcast sky, no bright white areas. No people, no vehicles, no pool, no text, no logo, no watermark, no UI. This is a fictional architectural concept, not an existing studio project. Output high resolution.

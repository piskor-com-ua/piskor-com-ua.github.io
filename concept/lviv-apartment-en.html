The design brief

Time together.
Room for yourself.

How can an apartment of around 98 m² accommodate shared living, work and rest? Its layout suggests this design brief: spaces for gathering should coexist with separate rooms for concentration and sleep.

Our solution

The project includes a kitchen and dining room, living room, separate study and bedroom, wardrobe and two bathrooms.

For your comfort

Each part of the day has its own place: a conversation around the table can continue while someone works in another room.

The layout challenge

Work nearby.
Rest in its own space.

Several functions had to fit within the existing apartment. The documentation includes a measured survey, demolition, new partitions and the resulting layout: the interior starts with the arrangement of rooms and furniture.

Our solution

The 18.22 m² study and 16.26 m² bedroom are separate rooms. Furniture and lighting positions are developed in the respective plans.

For your comfort

The desk has a room of its own. The bedroom retains a setting for rest, with a bed, armchair and local lighting.

The storage solution

A place for belongings.
A character for the room.

Storage runs through the project, from the entrance hall to the kitchen and study. The visual challenge is to make large furniture volumes part of a coherent room composition.

Our solution

Bespoke furniture drawings were developed. Timber fronts alternate with a mirror in the hall; tall kitchen units for appliances join the worktop and upper cabinets.

For your comfort

Everyday belongings can be distributed among enclosed compartments, while the fronts and materials contribute to the interior.

Creating atmosphere

Light brings out
the material.

Blue, timber and burgundy surfaces have different depths and qualities. General, local and decorative lighting bring them together. Fixture positions are considered alongside furniture and finishes.

Our solution

Fixture and switching plans support cabinet, display and detail lighting. Wall elevations coordinate furniture, mouldings and lighting points.

For your comfort

Light draws attention to the part of the room in use: shelves, a mirror or a working area. Smaller details contribute to the overall atmosphere.

The design outcome

One apartment.
A considered day.

The outcome is an interior design project that brings together layout, furniture, finishes and service connection points. It moves from the overall vision to drawings, wall elevations and specifications for the next stages of work.

Our solution

Within 97.72 m², the design provides places to gather, work, sleep and store belongings. The wardrobe and bathrooms carry the same material and colour ideas through the apartment.

For your comfort

You can see the proposed home as a whole and discuss specific decisions, from cabinet placement to lighting beside a mirror.
